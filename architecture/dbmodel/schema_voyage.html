Voyage & Compliance Schemas

The voyage schema covers port calls, sea passages and route waypoints with PostGIS geometry. The compliance schema covers PSC inspections with deficiency codes, vessel certificates, and sanctions lists spanning vessels, companies and individuals.

7 tables total voyage.route_waypoint: PostGIS geometry psc_inspection ← psc_inspections.json sanction: vessel | company | person multi-FK joins to vessel + port

voyage Schema Port calls, passages, route waypoints

voyage.voyage

Purpose: One row per voyage (port-to-port passage). Normalised from movements.json top-level records. A voyage has a departure port, arrival port, cargo, charterer and status. Port calls and waypoints are child records. FK to both vessel.entity and port.entity.

voyage.port_call

Purpose: Individual port call events — one row per vessel stop. Mirrors port_calls.json. A voyage (port-to-port) may have multiple port calls (bunker stops, crew changes, transit through intermediate ports). Both imo_number and unlocode are indexed for lookups from either direction. Port_name is denormalised for PSC reporting convenience.

voyage.route_waypoint

Purpose: Individual waypoints on a voyage route. Normalised from movements.json nested "route" arrays. Each row is one stop (done/current/future) with PostGIS geometry for map rendering. seq_no determines the order of waypoints within a voyage. Mirrors the Movements.jsx timeline UI.

compliance Schema PSC inspections, certificates, sanctions

compliance.psc_inspection

Purpose: One row per Port State Control inspection. Directly mirrors psc_inspections.json — external_id matches the JSON "id" field (e.g. 'PSC-2024-0130-001'). Tracks detention status, deficiency count, MOU region and PSC authority. Child records in psc_deficiency capture the individual deficiency codes. Drives the PSC page and analytics.

compliance.psc_deficiency

Purpose: Individual deficiency records within a PSC inspection. One inspection → zero or more deficiency rows. Mirrors psc_inspections.json "deficiencies" array. deficiency_code matches international PSC code tables (e.g. '07106' = Fire dampers). Drives the deficiency category chart in the PSC page.
